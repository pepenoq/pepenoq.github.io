Six 50-question practice sets (300 questions total) covering NUR 504 Exam 4 — Week 9 (Neuro), Week 10 (Endocrine) and Week 11 (Musculoskeletal).

Set 1 is traumatic brain injury and increased intracranial pressure — GCS severity, the CSF halo sign, early versus late signs and Cushing’s triad, posturing, ICP nursing care, CPP and MAP calculations, mannitol and 3% saline math and safety, hemorrhagic stroke and craniotomy. Set 2 is spinal cord injury — spinal versus neurogenic shock and when they coexist, autonomic dysreflexia, the halo vest, SCI complications and Guillain-Barré. Set 3 is the diabetes review — diagnostic thresholds, insulin timing, insulin when NPO, hypoglycemia and the rule of 15, sick-day rules — and hyperglycemic hyperosmolar state. Set 4 is DKA — ABGs and the anion gap, ketones, the potassium thresholds, the insulin drip and its math, when to add dextrose, cerebral edema and resolution. Set 5 is diabetes insipidus and SIADH — reading sodium, osmolality, specific gravity and urine output together, desmopressin, fluid restriction, 3% saline and the sodium speed limit. Set 6 is fractures — traction, fixation and pin care, cast complications, fat embolism versus clot embolism, and compartment syndrome.

Every set leans on priority questions (“which client would the nurse assess first?”) and on reading lab values and vital-sign trends, and the calculation formats from the instructor’s practice questions appear throughout. Ten case-study chains built from the course activities follow one patient across several consecutive questions — a motorcycle-crash TBI worked through the CPP and mannitol worksheets, a C5 diving injury from the neurogenic versus spinal shock worksheet, ascending Guillain-Barré weakness, the nursing-home HHS case, an insulin-given-breakfast-held low, the DKA “Mr. J” worksheet, the Michael Turner SIADH case, post-pituitary-surgery DI, a femoral fracture with fat embolism, and the tibial compartment syndrome case.

Following this exam’s own question prompt, every item is written at the Application level or higher and is drawn only from the uploaded Exam 4 material — the lecture decks and their speaker notes, the Iggy study guides and answer keys, the worksheets and case studies, and the instructor’s practice questions. Each rationale explains why the correct answer is correct and lists why every other option is not the best answer, then names the Bloom’s level. Progress saves automatically in your browser.
