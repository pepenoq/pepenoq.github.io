Exam 2 — Respiratory & Renal Critical Care

Six 50-question NCLEX-style sets built from the Week 4, Week 5 and Week 6 lecture decks and speaker notes, the study chart and key-highlight sheets, and the in-class activities and case studies — ARDS, the trauma pneumothorax, the Renal NGN and the Respiratory COVID NGN. Every item is written at the Application level or higher: it asks what the nurse should do, not what a term means. No filler options — each distractor is a real finding, drug or intervention from an adjacent concept. Every rationale explains why the right answer is right and why the tempting wrong one is not, and names the Bloom’s level.

Set 1 · Week 4Respiratory assessment, thoracentesis, effusion, pneumothorax & tension
Set 2 · Week 4Chest tubes: chambers, tidaling, air leaks, dressings, emergencies
Set 3 · Week 4ABGs: the four disorders, compensation, hypoxemia, interventions
Set 4 · Week 5ARF, ARDS & refractory hypoxemia, PEEP & proning, PAH, COVID-19
Set 5 · Week 5Intubation, vent modes & alarms, weaning, VAP bundle, RASS & sedation
Set 6 · Week 6CKD & ESRD diet, HD access, PD & peritonitis, CRRT, transplant
