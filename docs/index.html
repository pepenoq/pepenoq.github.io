Exam 1 — Cardiovascular Critical Care

Four 50-question NCLEX-style practice exams built from the Week 1, Week 2 and Week 3 lecture decks, pre-class materials and in-class case studies. Written as hard critical-thinking items — multiple choice and select-all-that-apply — with no filler options: every distractor is a real drug, rhythm, value or intervention from an adjacent concept, so you have to know the material to rule it out. Every question carries a full rationale that also explains the trap.

Exam 1 · Week 1Hemodynamics, MAP & CVP, arterial and PA lines, pressors & inotropes, ECG basics
Exam 2 · Week 2Sinus, atrial & ventricular rhythms, blocks, antiarrhythmics, pacemakers & AICD
Exam 3 · Week 2Angina to STEMI, troponins, lead localization, PCI, fibrinolytics, CABG
Exam 4 · Week 3Valves & murmurs, endo/myo/pericarditis, tamponade, cardiomyopathy, aneurysm
