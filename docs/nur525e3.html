Six practice sets (168 questions total) covering NUR 525 Exam 3 — Weeks 7 through 10, built around the Exam 3 Study Guide.

Set 1 is vulnerable populations, poverty and homelessness — health equity versus equality, what creates vulnerability, health disparities, food deserts, poverty as the “determinant of the determinants,” the assistance programs, wraparound services, and the health risks and barriers facing people who are unhoused. Set 2 is teen pregnancy, mental illness and rural and migrant health — why every teen pregnancy is high risk, the mature minor doctrine, education as the long-term intervention, rural barriers and professional isolation, migrant occupational hazards, acute pesticide toxicity, teach-back and interpreter use. Set 3 is alcohol, tobacco and other drugs — drug, set and setting, the drug categories, nicotine dependence and cessation, methamphetamine, the cycle of addiction, enabling, opioid overdose and naloxone, and harm reduction. Set 4 is violence, human abuse and safety — control as the purpose of family violence, abuse versus neglect, intimate partner violence, human trafficking, suicide risk, Massachusetts mandated reporting, restraints, falls, RACE and PASS. Set 5 is Week 10 — school nursing, occupational health and faith community nursing. Set 6 is Week 9 — bioterrorism and disaster management, from the Category A agents and their precautions to the four disaster phases, the response agencies, and START and JumpSTART triage.

Six case-study chains run across the sets, where several consecutive questions follow one situation as it develops: a family shelter, a migrant farmworker, a home visit that becomes an overdose, a clinic visit with a controlling companion, the Wayne County school health consultation, and a hurricane evacuation shelter.

Every item is drawn only from the Exam 3 material — the Study Guide, the weekly objectives, the lecture decks, the Are You Ready worksheets and quiz, and the School Nursing case study. The levels of prevention run through every set, because the Study Guide treats them as the master key to the whole exam. Each rationale explains why the correct answer is correct and lists why every other option is not the best answer, then names the Bloom’s level. Progress saves automatically in your browser.
